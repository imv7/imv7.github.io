Autonomous Script Selection 🏁

US 2022/0188213 A1 · Selecting Automation Scripts Using Reinforced Learning · Kyndryl · Manhaes, Das Neves, Varga, Monteiro Vieira, Todari

An incident fires and the system fixes it without waking anyone. Candidate scripts are scouted, raced against each other in a cloned replica of production, and the winner is deployed straight to real production. There is no approval step, no ticket, no operator in the path. Every move costs penalty points: waiting −2, crashing −3, failing to fix it −9. Closest to zero wins and ships.

Nothing here is scripted in advance. Each incident is synthesised on the spot from a fault class, a host, a blast radius and four independently drawn candidate behaviours. Press AUTOPILOT and incidents keep arriving, one after another, with no gap and no clicks.
