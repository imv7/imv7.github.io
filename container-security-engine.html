Autonomous Multi-Cloud Placement

US 12,112,155 B2 · Software Application Container Hosting · Kyndryl · Monteiro Vieira et al.

The engine deploys containers across several clouds with no human in the loop. It reads the build file, scores the image against the patent's monotonicity conditions, and places the workload on whichever cloud its own controls make safest — or refuses it outright. Nobody approves anything.

Nothing here is hand-picked. Every admission request below is synthesised on the spot from a combinatorial sampler, so the queue you are looking at has never existed before and will not repeat. Press AUTOPILOT and the engine runs unattended, admitting workload after workload until you stop it.
